O.V.O. is a four-legged robot I modelled, rigged and animated in Blender. The story: it's on a mission near Earth, gets sent a camera to check everything's fine… and decides to take selfies with it.

O.V.O. 是我在 Blender 中建模、绑定并制作动画的四足机器人。故事是：它在地球附近运行任务，收到一台用来确认一切正常的相机……然后决定拿来自拍。
